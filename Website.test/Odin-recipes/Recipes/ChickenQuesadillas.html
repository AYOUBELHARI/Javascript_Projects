Chicken Quesadillas

Description

Chicken quesadillas are a delicious and easy-to-make Mexican dish that consists of a tortilla filled with cooked chicken, cheese, and other ingredients. They are typically cooked on a griddle or in a skillet until the tortilla is crispy and the cheese is melted. Chicken quesadillas can be served as an appetizer, snack, or main course, and they are often accompanied by salsa, guacamole, or sour cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or a blend)
  • 1/2 cup diced onions
  • 1/2 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • Cooking spray or a small amount of oil for cooking

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, shredded cheese, diced onions, diced bell peppers, chopped cilantro, ground cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine all the ingredients.
  2. Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly coat it with cooking spray or a small amount of oil.
  3. Place one tortilla on the skillet and spread a portion of the chicken mixture evenly over half of the tortilla.
  4. Fold the other half of the tortilla over the filling to create a half-moon shape.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, or until the tortilla is golden brown and crispy, and the cheese is melted.
  6. Remove the quesadilla from the skillet and let it cool for a minute before slicing it into wedges.
  7. Repeat the process with the remaining tortillas and filling.
  8. Serve the chicken quesadillas warm with your favorite dipping sauces such as salsa, guacamole, or sour cream.
